The story

Wednesday Addams starts a new chapter of her life at Nevermore Academy, a school for students with unusual abilities.

During her time at the academy, Wednesday becomes involved in a series of mysterious events and begins investigating what is happening around her.

This page brings together information about the story, characters, timeline and some interesting details from the series.
